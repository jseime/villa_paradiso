The chapel

Say your vows beneath painted vaults

At the heart of the estate stands a small consecrated chapel, its vaulted ceiling covered in frescoes and its doors opening onto palms and old trees.

It seats around twenty of your closest guests for an intimate ceremony, while everyone else gathers on the lawn outside. Because the chapel is consecrated, a Catholic ceremony is possible here, subject to the agreement of your priest and the local diocese.

Few venues in central Italy offer a private chapel that is used only by the people who rent the estate on that day.

The estate

Space, shade and stone

Ten hectares of private countryside on the edge of Aquapendente, reached by its own drive and entirely yours for the day.

The stone villa

Painted ceilings, terracotta floors, antique furniture and two bathrooms, including a clawfoot tub. A beautiful, private place for the couple to get ready.

The lawn

A wide lawn beside the villa, framed by mature trees, with room for a reception of up to 150 guests under a marquee or the open sky.

The lime-tree drive

A long drive lined with lime trees, with a second tree-lined avenue beyond it. A natural aisle for an outdoor ceremony, a dramatic arrival, or an unforgettable photo walk.

The Etruscan caves

Beneath the villa lie ancient Etruscan caves, their arched openings set in a wild garden with an old stone basin, half reclaimed by nature. Perfect for portraits and atmospheric scenes.

Painted interiors

Frescoed ceilings and period rooms inside the villa, for getting-ready photographs and interior scenes.

Practical by design

Plenty of parking, water and power on site, and a drive that lets trucks reach the lawn, so your suppliers can set up with ease.
